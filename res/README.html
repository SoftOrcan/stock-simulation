Stock Simulator Features

  • Calculate the net profit/loss of a stock from and to a specified date range.
  • Calculate the x-day moving average of a stock over a specified time period. The x-day moving average is the average of the last x days (starting from the given date). Note: This corresponds to the last x days when the stock prices are available.
  • Calculate the x-day crossovers of a stock over a specified time period. An x-day crossover happens when the closing price for a day is greater than the x-day moving average for that day. A crossover signals a "buy" opportunity. A crossover can be defined over any period of time, although the 30-day period (i.e. the 30-day crossover) is usually popular.
  • Create a portfolio with a specified name. A portfolio is utilized to contain shares of one or more stock.
  • Add a stock to a portfolio, representative of purchasing a share of a stock to a portfolio.
  • Remove a stock from a portfolio.
  • Calculate the value of a portfolio on a specific date. Calculate by identifying the value of each stock in the portfolio on the specified date multiplied by the quantity of the respective stock.
  • Quit the application.
